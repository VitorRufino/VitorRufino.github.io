Curiosidades Diárias

One short educational video every day about science, mathematics, history and geography, written, narrated and illustrated with the help of AI and reviewed by the creator before it is posted on TikTok.

How it works

  1. 1Topic and scriptA new topic is chosen each day and an AI model writes a 60 to 90 second script, followed by a fact-checking pass.
  2. 2Narration and visualsThe script is narrated with a synthetic voice and illustrated with images, formulas, charts and timelines.
  3. 3Video assemblyEverything is put together into a vertical video with large, word-by-word captions.
  4. 4Draft in TikTokThe finished video is sent to the creator's TikTok inbox as a draft. The creator reviews, edits and decides whether to post it.

Connecting a TikTok account

The creator connects their own TikTok account once, using TikTok's official authorization page (Login Kit). The app asks for two permissions:

  • user.info.basic to identify which TikTok account was connected.
  • video.upload to send finished videos to that account's TikTok inbox as drafts.

The app never publishes anything on its own. Every video waits in the TikTok inbox until the creator opens it in the TikTok app, reviews it, adds the caption and the AI-generated content label, and chooses to post it. It does not read the account's videos, followers, comments or messages.

Access can be removed at any time in the TikTok app's settings for connected apps. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Who runs it

Curiosidades Diárias is run by an independent content creator in Brazil to produce the educational videos for their own TikTok channel.

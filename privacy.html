Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Curiosidades Diárias (the "Service") handles, why, and the choices you have. The Service is operated by Vitor, an independent content creator based in Brazil ("we", "us"), who is the controller of the information described here.

1. Information we handle

From TikTok, when an account is connected

  • Account identifier (open_id), which identifies the connected TikTok account within the Service.
  • Access token and refresh token, which allow the Service to upload drafts to that account.
  • The list of permissions granted (user.info.basic and video.upload).

The user.info.basic permission can also make basic profile details such as display name and avatar available. The Service does not request or store them.

When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and the content of your message.

When you visit this website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may process technical data such as IP addresses to deliver and secure the site, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

2. How we use information

  • To upload each finished video to the connected account's TikTok inbox as a draft, where the account owner reviews it and decides whether to post it.
  • To keep the connection working by refreshing the access token.
  • To answer messages and requests sent to us.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not publish content automatically.
  • We do not read the connected account's videos, followers, comments, likes or messages.
  • We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Service providers

The Service relies on the following providers. They receive the content needed to create and deliver the videos (topics, scripts, narration, images and the final video file), not TikTok account data, except where stated:

  • GitHub (Microsoft) runs the daily automation and stores the TikTok tokens as encrypted secrets.
  • Anthropic writes and fact-checks the scripts.
  • OpenAI generates the narration, word timings and images.
  • TikTok receives the finished videos as drafts in the connected account.
  • Telegram (optional) sends the creator a notification with the video and caption.

5. Storage and security

TikTok tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in a private GitHub repository and are only accessible to the automation that uploads drafts. All communication with TikTok and other providers uses HTTPS. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect this information.

6. Retention

  • Tokens are kept while the account remains connected. TikTok access tokens expire after 24 hours and refresh tokens after up to 365 days; revoked or expired tokens stop working immediately.
  • Generated videos are kept in the automation's temporary storage for up to 14 days.
  • A list of topics already covered is kept so that topics are not repeated. It contains no personal information.
  • Emails are kept for as long as needed to handle the request.

7. Your choices and rights

You can disconnect the Service at any time in the TikTok app's settings for connected apps. You can also email us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information. When you disconnect or ask us to delete your data, we remove the stored tokens and identifiers within 30 days. If you are in Brazil, you have the rights provided by the Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados (LGPD), and you may also contact the Brazilian data protection authority (ANPD).

8. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and only TikTok users who meet TikTok's minimum age requirements can connect an account.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed.
